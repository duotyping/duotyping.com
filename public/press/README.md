Everything here is free to use in coverage of DuoTyping. Download it all at once:
<https://duotyping.com/press/duotyping-press-kit.zip>

## About DuoTyping

**Short:** DuoTyping is a writing assistant for Mac, Windows, Chrome and the web. It checks grammar
and tone in the apps you already write in, and changes nothing until you accept.

**Long:** DuoTyping is a writing assistant with apps for Mac and Windows, an extension for Chrome
and Edge, and a web app that works on phones. Select text in Mail, Outlook, Slack or your browser,
press one shortcut, and DuoTyping suggests fixes for spelling, grammar, punctuation and tone,
sentence by sentence or as a whole rewrite. Nothing changes until you accept. It checks with
DuoTyping’s own free checks, which DuoTyping keeps, encrypted, to improve DuoTyping, or with a
cloud model on your own API key (OpenAI, Anthropic or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint) in the Mac
and Windows apps, and then DuoTyping stores none of what you write. A free account gives you more
checks each day, and the extension and the web app use one.

## Using the logo

- The colour logo is the app icon beside the name. Use it on light backgrounds and the
  `-on-dark` version on dark ones. Over a photo or a busy background, use the one-colour black
  or white version.
- Don't show the colour bubbles without their tile; on their own, use the one-colour mark.
- Leave clear space around the logo at least the height of the "D" in DuoTyping.
- Don't recolour, rotate, stretch, outline or add effects to the logo, and don't redraw the
  wordmark in another typeface.
- Two bubbles: yours behind in terracotta, the assistant's in front in midnight with two tall
  cyan eyes. Don't draw the assistant's bubble on its own.
